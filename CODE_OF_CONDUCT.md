# DjGoo community code of conduct

DjGoo contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces professional, technically focused, and safe for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, behavior, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than attacking people.
- Give reproducible details and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Accept review without retaliation and explain disagreements with technical reasoning.
- Respect privacy, consent, credentials, and unpublished security information.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption.
- Do not pressure users to share private Discord access, remote-computer access, microphone recordings, tokens, keys, or personal information.
- Keep automated or AI-assisted contributions attributable and reviewable; the contributor remains responsible for correctness, licensing, and security.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments, issues, reviews, or contributions that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Reports should be made privately to the repository owner through the GitHub profile associated with this project. Include links, dates, and relevant context. Do not publicly repost harmful material merely to prove it exists.

Maintainers should handle reports consistently, protect reporters from unnecessary exposure, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate the outcome when appropriate. False reports made in good faith are not violations; knowingly fabricated reports may be.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release channels, project-operated relay services, and public interactions where someone is representing DjGoo.
